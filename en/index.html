In Denmark · free

Forbrugeragenten

A free app for people in Denmark that takes care of electricity, mobile and internet: it writes to all the providers in your categories, collects offers, gathers your bills in one place and helps you get money back from your old electricity company. You just say yes.

iPhone and AndroidGet the app for free

How it handles electricity, mobile and internet

Ready
  1. 1YouConfirm with MitIDYou confirm with MitID, Denmark’s digital ID, so the agent can write to the providers for you.
  2. 2The agentGathers your contracts and bills in one placeElectricity, mobile and internet: contracts, lock-in periods and bills in one app.
  3. 3The agentWrites to the providers and collects offersTo all the providers in your categories, so you don’t have to talk to a salesperson.
  4. 4The agentLines up the offers by price, terms and qualityIt compares the offers on price, terms and quality.
  5. 5YouChoose one of the offersForbrugeragenten isn’t owned by any provider and works only for you.
  6. 6The agentHandles contact with the providers afterwardsAll communication is gathered in one inbox in the app. You order some offers yourself.
  7. 7The agentGets money back from your old electricity companyIf you’re owed money after a switch, it asks for it on your behalf. It costs you nothing.

Agent360 OS

on the way

For companies · going open source

Your company’s new internal operating system.

Every company already has one: who the customer is, what was promised and what needs to happen next. Usually it’s scattered across emails, spreadsheets and people’s heads. We believe the company of the future gathers it in one place, so the AI knows the context and can prepare the work.

How it works

  1. Your teamAn overview across everything, and agents that prepare the work. A person says yes before anything is sent.
  2. Agent360 OSTies customers, email, meetings, knowledge and customer portals together. If you’re missing a tool, you should be able to describe it in plain Danish, and the system builds it.
  3. Your toolsThe email, CRM, phone system and documents you use today. You own your data.

Agent360 uses it in-house today, and the code is going open source. People on the waiting list get early access.

Before Agent360

I’ve been an entrepreneur since 2020 and have worked with tech and software: various webshops and, in 2023, EcommerceBot, an AI platform for Shopify stores with customer chat, ad testing and social media in one place. From 2021 to 2025 I ran my own consulting company, which I sold in July 2025.

What I learned from EcommerceBot

I didn’t continue with EcommerceBot. I think tools for webshops will have a really hard time, because Shopify is, over time, building its own system of AI agents into the platform, so most of it will come from Shopify itself. There will still be room for tools that specialise in one specific thing. But a broad platform like EcommerceBot has no place in that market unless it becomes a competitor to Shopify.
